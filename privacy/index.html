Privacy Notice

How we handle the information you send us, and what we do not do with it.

Who is responsible

The Holzapfel Group, Cape Town, Western Cape, South Africa, is the responsible party (POPIA) and data controller (GDPR) for the information described here. Contact: tim@holzapfel.co.za.

What we collect

Only what you type into the enquiry form, plus the minimum needed to keep the form working:

  • Your enquiry: name, email address, and optionally a telephone number, the dates and route of your visit, the service you are interested in, and anything else you write in the message field.
  • Technical data: when you send the form, our form delivery service receives your IP address and the time of submission, which it uses to deliver your message and to block automated abuse.

This website sets no cookies, runs no analytics, loads no third party scripts, and embeds no social media or advertising trackers. Fonts are served from our own domain, so no font provider receives your IP address.

Why we collect it, and on what basis

Solely to respond to your enquiry and, if you engage us, to arrange the service. Under POPIA we process it with your consent, given by ticking the consent box, and because it is necessary to conclude or perform a contract with you. Under the GDPR the corresponding bases are Article 6(1)(a) consent and Article 6(1)(b) steps taken at your request prior to entering a contract.

We do not use your details for marketing unless you separately ask us to.

Who sees it

When you press Send, your enquiry is transmitted to Web3Forms, a form delivery service operated from India, which forwards it by email to our mailbox. Our email is hosted by Airpark in South Africa. Both act as operators (processors) on our behalf. Web3Forms works with providers such as Amazon Web Services and Cloudflare and may process your information outside South Africa or the EEA; where it does, transfers are covered by its data processing agreement, including the EU Standard Contractual Clauses.

We do not sell, rent or share your information with anyone else, and we do not disclose client identities. Requests from law enforcement are answered only where we are legally obliged to do so.

How long we keep it

Enquiries that do not become bookings are deleted within 12 months. Where you become a client, records are kept for as long as required by South African tax and company law, ordinarily five years from the end of the relevant tax period.

Web3Forms keeps a copy of each submission for a limited period under its own retention policy, currently 30 days on our plan, and then deletes it.

Your rights

You may ask us for a copy of the information we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it, object to our processing it, or withdraw your consent at any time. Withdrawing consent does not affect processing already carried out. Write to tim@holzapfel.co.za and we will respond within the period the law allows.

If you are not satisfied, you may complain to the Information Regulator of South Africa (inforegulator.org.za) or, if you are in the EEA or the UK, to your local supervisory authority.

Security

The site is served over HTTPS only, and the enquiry form is sent to Web3Forms over an encrypted connection. Your enquiry is not stored on this website.
